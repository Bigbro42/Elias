**Slide 20, replace completely:**
"To conclude and to answer our research question. Norway is rich, well governed and highly collaborative, but its growth is below average, and its knowledge base is not yet turning into enough new products, intellectual property and exports. Hywind and Northern Lights show that technical validation is not enough. Both moved only once a committed buyer was in place. ECN can make that path systematic: one national pipeline, gates that advise the funders, and measurement based on outcomes. We are also clear about our limits: the innovation indicators are partly shaped by petroleum exports, both cases are state-backed, and our only interview was with ECN itself. We started with Porter’s sentence that prosperity is created, not inherited. ECN’s CEO told us that integration and funding will decide whether ECN is a success. Our answer adds one test: success means first orders. Prosperity is created, not inherited, one order at a time. Thank you for your attention. We look forward to your questions."
